# Funnel structures

Each journey is a default order. Drop any section you lack truthful content for. Never pad.
★ = usually essential.

## Webinar registration
★Hero (event title as promise, date/time with timezone, `Countdown` to start, CTA) ➜
★What you'll learn (3–5 specific takeaways) ➜ Who this is for / not for ➜
Problem ➜ desired outcome ➜ ★About the host (authority) ➜ Proof (only real testimonials or results) ➜
FAQ (time zone, replay availability, cost, what to bring) ➜ ★Final CTA (repeat date + countdown).
Registration = client's GHL form embed (inline section or modal), or CTA to their registration URL.

## Free training registration
Same as webinar, but on-demand: no countdown unless there's a real deadline. Emphasise
"watch instantly", runtime, and format. Hero may show a still or trailer of the training.

## VSL (video sales letter)
★Pre-headline (who it's for) ➜ ★Headline ➜ ★Video (large, centred, click-to-load facade,
thumbnail from client) ➜ ★CTA below the video (optionally revealed after a configured
time via `revealAfterSeconds`, but only if the client asks) ➜ Key points recap ➜ Proof ➜
Offer breakdown ➜ Guarantee (only if real) ➜ FAQ ➜ Final CTA.
Keep distractions out of the hero: no nav, minimal elements around the video.

## High-ticket application
★Hero (who it's for + the transformation + "Apply" CTA) ➜ Qualifier ("this is for you
if… / not for you if…") ➜ Problem and cost of inaction ➜ ★Unique mechanism / how it works (steps) ➜
What's included ➜ ★About the expert ➜ Proof ➜ Process (apply ➜ call ➜ decision) ➜ FAQ ➜
★Application section (GHL survey/form embed or CTA to the application URL).
Tone: selective, calm, premium. No hype stickers or fake scarcity.

## Booking / strategy call page
★Hero (what the call is, who it's for, what they leave with) ➜ ★Calendar embed (GHL
booking widget) high on the page ➜ What happens on the call (3 steps) ➜ Host ➜ FAQ.
On mobile, the calendar should be reachable within one scroll.

## Lead magnet
★Hero split: promise + ★opt-in form (or CTA to modal) beside a ★render of the asset
(client mockup, or a CSS/SVG mockup you build) ➜ What's inside (bullets) ➜ Who it's for ➜
Author ➜ Repeat opt-in. Short page.

## Challenge registration
★Hero (challenge name, outcome, dates, `Countdown` to day 1) ➜ ★Day-by-day agenda
(timeline component) ➜ What you'll walk away with ➜ Host ➜ Community/format (live, group,
prizes, but only if real) ➜ Price/VIP upgrade if any (`OfferStack`) ➜ FAQ ➜ ★Final CTA.

## Thank-you page (noindex)
★Confirmation headline ("You're registered" / "Check your email") ➜ ★Next steps (numbered:
add to calendar, check inbox/whitelist, join community, watch a pre-roll video) ➜
Optional: a welcome video from the host, a booking embed or a low-ticket offer if the client provides one.
Do not repeat a sales page. Add a "add to calendar" link only with real event details.

## Webinar replay (noindex)
★Headline + ★replay video (top) ➜ CTA under video (offer/application/booking) ➜ Optional
replay-expiry `Countdown` (real deadline only) ➜ Offer recap ➜ FAQ ➜ CTA.

## Hero recipes

Choose one, then make it yours.

1. **Editorial split.** Headline left (7 columns), portrait or asset right (5 columns) bleeding off
   the edge. Thin rules and an eyebrow above the headline. Good for coaching and authority.
2. **Centred stage.** Centred headline under a `Spotlight`, with the CTA and event meta inline
   below. Countdown in a glass pill. Good for webinars and challenges.
3. **Video-first.** Short pre-headline + headline over a large 16:9 video frame that
   overlaps the next section. Good for VSLs and replays.
4. **Full-bleed photograph.** Graded photography, headline anchored bottom-left, meta in a
   frosted bar. Good for luxury, real estate and lifestyle.
5. **Asset showcase.** Promise + form left, a floating 3D-ish mockup (CSS perspective) right.
   Good for lead magnets.
6. **Typographic poster.** Oversized headline as the image, with minimal everything else. Good for
   bold or creator brands.

Every hero carries: an eyebrow or announcement, the headline, a clear subheadline, the
relevant details (date/time/format), one primary CTA, and an authority marker (host name
and role, a real logo row, or a real stat), all *without* clutter.
